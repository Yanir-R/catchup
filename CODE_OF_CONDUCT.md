# Code of conduct

This is a small project. Be kind and be useful.

**Expected:** be respectful, assume good faith, give specific and honest feedback, and accept that the maintainer has the last word on what goes into the project.

**Not accepted:** harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, sharing someone's private information, and spam.

**What happens:** the maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, lock threads, and block people who break these rules.

**Report a problem:** use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment or issue, or open a private report on the Security tab.
